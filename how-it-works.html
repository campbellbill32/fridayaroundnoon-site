How a Friday gets here

Cold beer, a cooler, and a knock that means the week can stop practicing.

  1. Pick a plan

    Desk Buddy, Meeting MVP, or Floor Legend. The names are silly. The prices are pretend. The beer, in this story, is cold.

  2. Tell us the drop spot

    Office, porch, or “the desk by the plant that is somehow still alive.” A real door code helps. “1234” does not impress the cooler.

  3. We roll up around noon

    Sun hat on, ice intact, timing described as “around noon” because we are a brand, not a train schedule.

  4. You take the credit

    We will not tell your coworkers you outsourced the charisma. We might tell the plant. The plant does not slack anyone.

The delivery zone

We deliver anywhere a cooler can arrive before the ice starts a union. Homes, offices, and porches with a chair are in. Hot-air balloons, the middle of a lake, and “I’m fully remote from a tent” are a fond no.

If your spot has a door and at least one person who said “someone should grab beers,” you are in the zone. The moon can wait for a bigger cooler.
