Questions we gladly made up

Is this a real delivery service?

No. Friday Around Noon is a fictional demo. The prices are jokes. Nobody is heading to your office with beer unless you invited an actual friend.

Do I have to be 21?

Yes. Even the pretend beer has a pretend bouncer. If you are under 21, this page is just a sunny story.

What if I am in a meeting at noon?

In the story, we leave the cooler with the front desk or with whoever looks most likely to share. The mystery yogurt already in the fridge is not our department.

Can I skip a Friday?

In this demo, skip it by not sending the form. In the fiction, tell us by Tuesday and the cooler takes the week off without taking it personally.

What if Friday is a holiday?

We pretend Thursday is Friday, or we both take the day and call it even. Holiday Fridays are a dropdown option, not a legal strategy.
