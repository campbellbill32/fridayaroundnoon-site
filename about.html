A made-up origin story

The founder of the empty fridge

Casey Noon is fictional. The lime in the office fridge, we fear, is not.

How the cooler got a job

Casey worked in an office where Friday plans were a group project with no leader. At 11:58 someone would stand up, pat their pockets, and announce they could “totally swing by the store.” At 12:07 the fridge still held one lime and a yogurt with handwriting nobody would claim.

So Casey bought a cooler, a sun hat that was doing too much, and a promise: cold beer, at the office or the house, every Friday around noon. No voting. No “I thought you were getting it.” Just a knock and a temperature.

The company never left the bit. There is no warehouse, no fleet, and no tab. Friday Around Noon is a practice website with a weekend personality. If you want a real beer, buy it yourself, be 21 or older, and drink it like a person with Monday upcoming.

Casey still checks the fridge in the story. The lime is still in there. It has seniority now.
